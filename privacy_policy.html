Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 July 2026 · Last updated: 1 July 2026

Lega ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Lega mobile application (the "App"), an AI-powered tourist guide for Osijek, Croatia. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information – email address and display name, collected when you register or sign in with Google.
  • Location data – approximate GPS location, collected only while the App is in use to personalise place recommendations and directions. We do not track location in the background.
  • Usage data – anonymised events (screens viewed, features used, XP earned) collected via Firebase Analytics to improve the App.
  • Crash reports – anonymised diagnostic data collected via Firebase Crashlytics when the App crashes or encounters an error.
  • Chat history – your conversations with the Lega AI guide are stored locally on your device and, when synced, on our secure backend servers to provide chat continuity.
  • Gamification data – XP, badges, visited places, and streaks associated with your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalise the App's features (recommendations, directions, chat).
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve App stability (crash reports).
  • To understand how the App is used and prioritise new features (analytics).

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) – authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and cloud storage.
  • Google Sign-In – optional sign-in method.
  • Anthropic Claude – powers the AI chat assistant. Chat messages are sent to Anthropic's API for processing and are subject to Anthropic's data usage policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from the App's Settings screen, which permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our servers. Firebase Authentication data is deleted immediately upon account deletion.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including HTTPS for all data in transit and Firebase Security Rules for cloud storage. Your authentication tokens are stored in encrypted secure storage on your device.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below or use the "Delete account" option in the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
